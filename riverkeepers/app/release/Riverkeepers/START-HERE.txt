RIVERKEEPERS — FULL GAME

Unzip this folder, then open index.html in a browser.
Use its links to open the two play sheets, rules, and complete play example.
Print both sheet pages one-sided, black and white, on A4 or Letter paper
at 100% scale. Turn off browser headers and footers. If an edge clips,
use your printer's fit-to-printable-area setting.

You need a pencil and two six-sided dice. The rules include a numbered
paper-slip substitute if you do not have dice. One game is about 20–30
minutes. The pages work offline and contain no external scripts.

The files are HTML and CSS, not PDF. Do not separate the HTML files from
their companion CSS files. You can save a PDF copy with your browser's
Print / Save as PDF command if you prefer.

For personal play, you may print copies and keep a backup. Do not resell,
redistribute, or upload the paid files publicly. Sale and refund terms are
on the Riverkeepers sales page.
