Hardship: If neither die allows a legal build, build nothing, mark +1 Hardship, and use the lower die for Water.

Future · The Keepers

Turns 1–8

Copy the Past map, then roll two dice each turn. Choose a Row (doubles: no choice) and spend the other die's Steps on roads. On doubles, resolve a Future Event first.

Hardship: If neither die assignment allows a legal road, draw nothing and mark +1 Hardship.
