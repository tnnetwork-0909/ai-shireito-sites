Future: The Keepers

For each pair, choose one die as the Row and turn the other into Steps. Write each listed road cell in order. A cell drawn earlier in the same turn can support the next one.

TurnDiceRow / StepsRoad and reason
14, 64 / 3E4 → E5 → F5, 1 Step each. E4 touches Embankment E3; F5 touches Town F6. The Bridge is now linked to that Town.
23, 23 / 1Enter C3 Ruins from Old Bridge D3. Mark r, gain 1 Relic. The network now reaches both banks.
33, 43 / 2B3 → B2. B3 touches road C3 and Town A3. B2 touches B3 and Town B1.
45, 65 / 3F4 → G5 → A4. F4 joins G4 to the eastern roads; G5 satisfies Row 5; A4 joins A5 to A3. These are three separate branches, each touching the network when drawn. Five Towns are now linked.
