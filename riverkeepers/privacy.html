Privacy policy

This policy describes the Riverkeepers website and digital game sale operated by TN network. Contact: tnnetwork.support@icloud.com.

Information we collect and why

  • If you choose to sign up for release news, you provide an email address. We use it to send Riverkeepers updates. You can unsubscribe from each email.
  • If you buy the game, Gumroad collects the information needed for checkout and digital delivery. We may receive purchase details, including your email address, to deliver the game, answer support requests and handle refunds. Payment card details are handled by Gumroad and its payment providers, not by this website.
  • If you email us, we receive your address and message and use them to answer you.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website. GitHub may process technical access information, such as IP address and browser data, to deliver and secure the site. This website does not run its own analytics, advertising trackers, or account system.

External services and sharing

The website is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub). The optional email form submits directly to Buttondown. Purchases and file delivery take place on Gumroad, which acts as merchant of record for sales. We use these services only for the purposes described above; we do not sell your email address or add a buyer to the Buttondown list without a separate sign-up.

These services process data under their own policies: GitHub, Buttondown, and Gumroad.

Retention and your choices

We keep support correspondence and purchase details only as needed to answer requests, maintain purchase records, and meet legal duties. You can unsubscribe from Riverkeepers emails at any time and request deletion of your address; Buttondown may retain records required by its policy or law. Gumroad and GitHub retain data under their own policies and legal duties. To ask about access, correction, or deletion of information held by us, email tnnetwork.support@icloud.com. For data held independently by a service, use its privacy contact as well.
