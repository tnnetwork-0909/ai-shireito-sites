Can you play print and play games offline?

Yes, if you have downloaded the complete rules and components and the game does not require a website or app during play. Print the needed sheets, gather any dice or cards, and check that you can read the rules with your connection off before you leave.

Check what the game needs

“Print and play” describes how you prepare a game; it does not guarantee that every part works offline. Read the contents and rules before relying on a downloaded sheet alone.

If a game asks you to generate each new sheet online, downloading one sheet may let you play that session offline but may not let you start another. Check the publisher's instructions for that particular game.

Test an offline session before you go

  1. Save the full game. Download and extract the files if they come in a ZIP. Keep linked rules and component files together in their original folders.
  2. Read the component list. Prepare every required sheet, die, card, and writing tool. If the game has tables or a score guide, decide whether to print them or keep them open locally.
  3. Disconnect and reopen the files. Turn off your connection for the test, then open the local start file or document. Follow its links to the rules and sheets; do not rely on an already loaded browser tab.
  4. Try the first action on a spare sheet. Confirm that the rules explain what to mark and where. Reconnect to get missing files before your real session, then start with a clean sheet.

A local test is especially useful when a page looks complete but loads a font, image, or rule table from elsewhere. The printed game itself needs no connection once all required information is on paper.

Riverkeepers: a checked local example

The full Riverkeepers download is a ZIP containing local HTML and CSS files. Its start page has Open the play sheets, Rules, and Play example links; these lead to files in the same download. The printable sheets and rules do not load external scripts, fonts, or stylesheets, so the complete game can be played after download without an account or connection. See the full game and its contents.

  1. Extract the ZIP and open index.html. Choose Open the play sheets and print both pages one-sided on Letter or A4 paper. Keep the Rules open locally, or print them.
  2. Take a pencil and two six-sided dice. Start on the Past sheet: eight turns of settlements, levees, bridges, Water, and floods. If you lack dice, the rules give a numbered-paper-slip substitute.
  3. After Past turn eight, use the conversion guide to copy the map to the Future sheet. If a Town survives, play eight Future road turns, then score the Main Network and record the ending.

For a concrete offline check, disconnect, reopen index.html from the extracted folder, and follow Rules to the Past Event table and Open the play sheets to the Water Track. If both appear, the information needed for those turns is available locally. The free browser decision preview is one fixed scene, not the downloadable full game.

FAQ

Does downloading a game sheet mean the whole game works offline?

Not always. The sheet may depend on rules, cards, a generator, or a companion app you have not saved. Check the component list and open every required file with your connection off.

Do I have to print the rules to play offline?

No, if the rules are saved locally and you can keep a device open while playing. Printing them is useful when you will not have a device available. In either case, make sure event tables and scoring instructions are accessible.

Can I play Riverkeepers offline after buying it?

Yes, after downloading and extracting the full ZIP. Open its local index.html for the play sheets, rules, and example; use paper, a pencil, and two dice for the complete game. The free browser preview is a separate, shorter scene.

Will a browser tab already open before I disconnect be enough?

It may show a cached page while its links or other files fail. Close that tab and reopen the saved local file with the connection off to test what you actually have. If a rule or component is missing, download it before play.
