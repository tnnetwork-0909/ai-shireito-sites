How to print a 4x6 postcard from a PDF

Confirm that the PDF page is 6 × 4 inches in landscape orientation, load 4 × 6 inch paper, and choose Actual size or 100% in the print dialog. Check the preview before printing: “Fit” can change the card’s physical size, while printer margins may clip content placed too close to an edge.

Read the print steps

Print the card at its intended size

  1. Check the PDF page. Open the file and confirm it is one landscape page measuring 6 × 4 inches. A PDF on Letter or A4 paper with a small card drawn in the middle needs a different print setup.
  2. Load the right paper. Put 4 × 6 inch paper in the printer as its instructions specify. In the print dialog, set the paper size to 4 × 6 inches, orientation to landscape, and media type to the paper you loaded.
  3. Keep the scale at 100%. Select Actual size or a custom scale of 100%. In Adobe Acrobat, Actual size prints without scaling, while Fit adjusts the page to the printable area.
  4. Inspect the preview. Make sure the entire photo and every word fit within the printer’s printable area. If the preview cuts off essential content, check the paper size and orientation first. Do not enlarge the PDF to fill the sheet.
  5. Make one test print. Compare the output with the preview before printing more copies. If size or cropping is wrong, change one print setting and test again.

The exact paper and media controls vary by printer. For example, Epson’s 4 × 6 photo paper instructions explicitly call for matching the media type and 4 × 6 paper size.

Using the photo postcard app

On a computer, the app’s Download print PDF button saves a one-page, landscape 6 × 4 inch file. Its card design keeps content inside a 0.25 inch safe margin, so the documented workflow is to print at Actual size on 4 × 6 inch photo paper; borderless mode is not required. Check your own printer’s preview because printable margins differ by device.

The PDF is a card front with a photo and your observation. It does not create an addressed or stamped postcard back, mail a physical card, or replace a care report. If you only need a text update, try the free visit note; for the photo card’s full details, see the main page.

FAQ

Should I choose Fit or Actual size for a 4x6 PDF?

Choose Actual size or 100% when the PDF page itself is 6 × 4 inches and the paper is 4 × 6 inches. Fit changes the page scale to match the printer’s reported printable area, which can make the finished card smaller.

Do I need borderless printing?

No for this app’s PDF: the designed card has a 0.25 inch safe margin and is intended for Actual size printing. Still check the preview because a printer’s nonprintable area can vary.

Why does the preview show a cut-off edge?

First check that paper size and orientation match the PDF. If they do, your printer may have a larger nonprintable margin or borderless expansion; test with the printer’s settings before printing more copies.

Can I mail the printed card as a postcard?

The app exports a single front page only, without an address or stamp layout on the reverse. If you plan to mail it, prepare the back separately and check the postal requirements for your destination.
