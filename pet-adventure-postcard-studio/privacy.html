Privacy policy

Effective October 20, 2026. This policy explains how TN network handles information related to this site, the free visit note, and Pet Adventure Postcard Studio.

Information and purpose

The free visit note uses the pet name, date, and observation you enter only to show, copy, or print your note in your browser. The paid studio uses your chosen photo and text only to render and download a card in your browser. These inputs are not sent to TN network, Gumroad, or Buttondown by either tool, and neither tool stores a visit history.

If you sign up for updates, your email address is sent by the form to Buttondown. We use it only for launch and product updates. You may unsubscribe from any update email or ask us to remove your address.

When you purchase through Gumroad, Gumroad processes the checkout and delivery. Gumroad may provide us your purchase contact details and order information so we can deliver the product, respond to support requests, and handle refunds. We do not collect card numbers on this site. If you email us, we use the information in your message to answer you.

Services and sharing

GitHub Pages hosts these public pages and may process technical access information, such as IP address and request details, to serve them. Buttondown processes newsletter signups and sends update emails. Gumroad processes purchases, digital delivery, and applicable transaction taxes. We share information with these services only for those purposes or where law requires it. We do not sell your personal information or add third-party advertising scripts to this site.

Each service also applies its own privacy policy: GitHub, Buttondown, and Gumroad.

Retention and choices

The tools keep your photo and note in the current browser session only; closing or reloading the page clears them. Buttondown holds signup information until you unsubscribe or ask us to remove it. Gumroad keeps order records under its own policies and applicable law. Email us to ask about information we hold or request correction or deletion where available.
