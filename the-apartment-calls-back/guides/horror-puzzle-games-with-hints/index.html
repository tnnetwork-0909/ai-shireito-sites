“Has hints” can mean anything from a vague tip to a full answer. If avoiding spoilers matters to you, look for help that reveals one level at a time rather than opening a walkthrough of every puzzle.

Check a hint system before you rely on it

  1. Find out where help lives: a game button, pause menu, journal, separate file, or outside walkthrough. Check whether it works without leaving the puzzle screen.
  2. Ask if it covers the final required puzzle too. A hint system that stops before the last gate may still leave you stuck.
  3. Look for a way to choose the amount of detail. If only a complete answer is offered, decide whether you would rather try a smaller clue from a friend first.
  4. When you use a hint, pause after the first level. Restate the claim in your own words, inspect the related evidence, and attempt one comparison before asking for more.

What staged help looks like in a call mystery

The free opening of The Apartment Calls Back begins with Answer the call and advances with Continue. It ends before the paid puzzles, so the hint system is part of the full edition. The game page shows a read-only excerpt of its call log without giving away a solution.

In the full edition, use Open the call log, select two lines or record details, and press Compare. If stuck, press Hint. For Call 1, the first hint is “Look at Call 1. Where does Jun say they are?” That identifies a place to inspect while leaving the comparison to you.

A later hint points to Jun's statement; the most specific hint names the two items to compare. You can stop before that final level if you want to keep the deduction. The button remains available while contradictions are unresolved, and the full game has a staged hint set for each of its three required contradictions.

FAQ

Are hints the same as a walkthrough?

Not always. A staged hint can point you toward evidence without giving the answer, while a walkthrough usually supplies the complete sequence. Check how much the first hint reveals before opening it.

Can I get a hint before making a wrong guess?

That depends on the game. In The Apartment Calls Back's full edition, Hint is available in the call log while a contradiction remains unsolved; you do not have to fail a comparison first.

Do the hints eventually reveal the answer?

In this game's full edition, the final hint for a contradiction identifies the pair to compare. Ask for earlier levels first if you prefer to make the deduction yourself.

Are hints included in the free opening?

No. The free opening is a scene you read, with no call-log puzzle to solve. The full edition contains the recorded calls, contradictions, and their hints.
