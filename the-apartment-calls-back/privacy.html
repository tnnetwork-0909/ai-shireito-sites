What information is handled

If you choose game updates about fixes and future releases, the signup link opens Buttondown's hosted page. Buttondown processes your email address and may collect subscription IP address, referrer, and email engagement information. We receive the information needed to manage the mailing list.

Gumroad handles checkout, payment details, receipt and download delivery. We can see the buyer and order information Gumroad makes available to sellers, such as the purchase email address and transaction details, so we can support your purchase and handle refunds. We do not collect card numbers on this site.

Gumroad hosts this sales page and the legal pages. GitHub Pages hosts only the free playable opening and may log preview visitors' IP addresses for security. The game itself needs no account. The full game stores puzzle progress in your browser's local storage on your device; it does not send that progress to us.

How information is used

We use signup emails only for game updates about fixes and future releases, and purchase information only to deliver the product, answer support requests, process refunds, and meet applicable record-keeping duties. We do not run advertising trackers on this site.

Services and sharing

We use Gumroad to host the sales and legal pages and handle purchases, Buttondown for opt-in email updates, and GitHub Pages for the free opening. Each service processes data needed to provide its service under its own privacy terms. We do not sell subscriber information or add buyers to the Buttondown list without a separate signup. Information may also be disclosed when required by law.

Choices and contact

You can unsubscribe using the link in any Buttondown email. You can clear game progress through the game's Start over control or your browser's site data settings. For access, correction, deletion, or questions about information we control, contact tnnetwork.support@icloud.com. Some transaction records may need to be retained for legal obligations.
