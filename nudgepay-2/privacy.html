Information we handle

The NudgePay tool asks for invoice and client details to create email drafts. These entries are stored only in your browser's local storage; the tool does not transmit them to us. You can remove them with the tool's Clear control. When you choose “Open in email app,” your browser passes the draft to your email application.

If you sign up for updates, Buttondown receives your email address and may record subscription time, IP address, and email delivery or engagement data. If you buy the optional offline copy, Gumroad collects checkout and purchase information. Gumroad makes order details available to us; we use them only as needed for delivery support, refunds, and records. If you contact us, we receive the information you put in your message. GitHub Pages processes technical requests, such as IP address, request time, and browser information, to deliver and secure the pages. We do not receive raw visitor logs from GitHub.

How we use information

We use signup email addresses only for launch notices and product updates, and to handle unsubscribe requests. Gumroad delivers the download; we use order details for transaction support, refunds, and required records. We use messages sent to us to answer the request. We do not use invoice entries for analytics or advertising, and this site loads no advertising or analytics scripts.

Our legal bases under UK GDPR

  • Signup emails: your consent (Article 6(1)(a)) for launch notices and product updates. You can withdraw it at any time by contacting us; withdrawing consent does not affect processing before withdrawal.
  • Purchase and related support details: performance of the purchase contract (Article 6(1)(b)) to provide the download and resolve delivery or refund requests. We retain records where needed to meet tax or accounting duties under legal obligation (Article 6(1)(c)).
  • Other messages to us: our legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f)) in answering questions and handling requests, balanced against the limited contact details needed to reply.

Service providers and sharing

Signup submissions go to Buttondown, purchases go to Gumroad, and site requests go to GitHub Pages. Buttondown processes the subscriber list on our behalf. Gumroad is an independent controller for payment, fraud prevention, and tax duties, and a processor for buyer information it holds on our behalf. GitHub explains its own processing of site request data in its privacy statement. We may disclose information when required by law. We do not sell signup email addresses.

Transfers outside the UK

Buttondown and Gumroad process information in the United States. For data they process on our behalf, their agreements use EU Standard Contractual Clauses with the UK Addendum for UK transfers: read Buttondown's agreement and Gumroad's addendum, or contact us for a copy of the applicable safeguards. Gumroad separately controls checkout data under its privacy policy. GitHub states that it uses the UK Extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework for personal data it receives from the UK.

Storage, retention and choices

Invoice entries remain in your browser until you clear them or your browser removes its storage. We keep active signup addresses until you unsubscribe or request deletion; Buttondown may retain a suppression record to prevent further messages. We keep purchase records for as long as needed for transactions, legal duties, and disputes. We keep support messages until the request is resolved and any related legal or dispute period ends. GitHub sets its own retention period for technical request data according to purpose and legal need. To unsubscribe, use the link in a Buttondown email or write to tnnetwork.support@icloud.com.

What you need to provide

Using the free tool does not require you to give us personal information. The email address for updates is optional; without it, we cannot send updates. Checkout details required by Gumroad are needed to complete a purchase; without them, you cannot buy the offline copy. Contacting us is optional, but we need a reply address to answer you. We do not use your information to make solely automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects.

Your UK data protection rights

You may ask us to access or correct your personal data, delete it where the right applies, restrict its use, or provide data you gave us in a portable form where applicable. You may withdraw consent for update emails at any time.

You have the right to object to direct marketing at any time. You may also object to processing based on our legitimate interests.

Email tnnetwork.support@icloud.com to exercise these rights or ask about your data. We will handle requests under applicable law. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).
