When an invoice is still open

14 Days Overdue Invoice Reminder Email for Freelancers

When a freelance invoice is 14 days overdue, verify the unpaid balance and send a direct follow-up with the invoice number, original due date and a clear next step. If an earlier reminder went unanswered, refer to it only if you actually sent it; set a reply deadline you can follow through on.

Email you can adapt

Subject: Invoice [invoice number] remains overdue — reply needed

Hi [client name],

Invoice [invoice number] for [outstanding amount and currency] was due on [full due date] and remains unpaid. I followed up on [date of earlier reminder].

Please arrange payment or reply by [reasonable calendar date] to explain what is holding it up. The payment instructions are [link or location on invoice]. If the invoice needs correcting, please tell me what is missing.

Thanks,
[your name]

Delete the sentence about an earlier reminder if this is your first contact. Use the actual outstanding balance if a partial payment arrived. Keep the reply date separate from any payment deadline in your agreement; this example asks the client to respond or pay, not to accept new terms.

Before sending the second follow-up

  1. Reconcile the invoice. Check bank or payment records, the original due date, any partial payment, and any message from accounts payable.
  2. Review the thread. Confirm what you previously sent and what the client promised. If they reported an error, resolve that first instead of escalating a template.
  3. Choose a specific next step. Ask for payment or a response by a full calendar date that you can monitor. Do not imply a legal or contractual consequence you cannot support.
  4. Send and keep a record. Reply in the existing invoice thread if possible and keep the invoice, messages and payment confirmations together.

If your main problem is a vague promise such as “soon,” use the guide to ask for a specific payment date. If this is the first message after the due date, start with the first overdue reminder.

FAQ

What should a 14-day overdue reminder include?

Include the invoice number, outstanding balance, original due date and one clear action. Add payment instructions or point to the attached invoice, and invite the client to report a blocker.

Can I say this is my second reminder?

Only if an earlier reminder was actually sent. If this is your first follow-up, remove that claim and use a factual opening.

Should I call it a final notice after 14 days?

The number of days alone does not make it a final notice. Use that label only if it matches your follow-up history and you are prepared for the next step. Do not add fees, legal threats or a work pause that your agreement does not support.

What if the client says there is a problem with the invoice?

Ask for the specific missing or incorrect detail and correct it before escalating your tone. Agree on the next payment step in writing so both sides know what happens next.
