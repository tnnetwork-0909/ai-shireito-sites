On the due date

Invoice Due Today Reminder Email for Freelancers

On the invoice due date, send a brief reminder that names the invoice, amount, full due date and way to pay. Say “due today,” not “overdue,” and invite the client to flag a payment problem before the day ends.

Email you can adapt

Subject: Invoice [invoice number] due today

Hi [client name],

Invoice [invoice number] for [amount and currency] is due today, [full due date]. Here are the payment details: [link or instructions].

If payment is already scheduled, thank you. Otherwise, could you arrange it today? If you need the invoice again or anything corrected, please reply and let me know.

Thanks,
[your name]

Replace every bracketed field. If the payment details are already clear on the attached invoice, point to that invoice instead of adding an unverified link. The message assumes payment has not yet been confirmed; check your records first.

Send a useful note in four steps

  1. Check the due date and balance. Use the date on the invoice and confirm that the amount has not already arrived. If the client paid part of it, state only the remaining balance.
  2. Check who handles payment. Send it to the agreed billing contact or reply in the original invoice thread. Attach the invoice again if the recipient may need it.
  3. Make one clear request. Ask for payment today, with the actual payment route or a reference to the invoice. Offer to fix missing billing details.
  4. Review before sending. If the client is in another time zone or the due date falls on a nonworking day for them, check whether a same-day request is reasonable under your agreement.

Match the wording to what you know

What to say when the invoice is due today
What you knowUseful wording
No payment confirmation“Could you arrange payment today?” Include the invoice reference and payment route.
Client says payment is scheduled“Thanks for the update. Please let me know if the payment date changes.” Do not say it is unpaid if you have not checked.
Client reports an invoice errorAsk what must be corrected and send the revised invoice to the right person. Agree on the next payment step.

If the due date passes and the balance is still open, use a first overdue reminder that states the original date rather than repeating “due today.” For a heads-up while the date is still ahead, see the before-due guide.

FAQ

Should I send a reminder on the invoice due date?

Yes, if payment is still pending and a short note would help the client act. Check your records and any earlier payment promise first, so you do not send an unnecessary reminder.

Is an invoice overdue on its due date?

Use “due today” while the invoice is still within the agreed due date. If it remains unpaid after that date, describe it as overdue and state the original due date.

What if the client says payment is already scheduled?

Thank them and note the date they gave you. Verify receipt through your own payment records before marking the invoice paid or sending another reminder.
