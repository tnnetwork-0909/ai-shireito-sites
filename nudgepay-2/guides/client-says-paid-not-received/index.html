When a client reports sending payment

Client Says They Paid but Payment Not Received: Email

If a client says they paid but you cannot see the payment, check your bank or payment provider and your invoice records first. Reply that you cannot match the payment yet, then ask for the send date, amount, method and transaction reference so both sides can trace it. Avoid saying the client did not pay or requesting a second transfer while the first one is being checked.

Email to request payment details

Subject: Checking payment for invoice [number]

Hi [client name],

Thanks for the update about invoice [number]. I have checked my records, but I cannot match a payment to it yet.

Could you send the date it was sent, the amount, the payment method and a transaction reference or remittance advice? I will compare those details with my bank or payment provider and let you know what I find.

If the payment is approved but has not actually been released, could you tell me when it is expected to be sent?

Thanks for helping me sort this out,
[your name]

Use “sent” only if the client says the transfer was actually released. If they said “approved,” “processed” or “scheduled,” ask what that status means in their payment system. Share only the details needed to identify the payment; avoid asking the client to email full account or card numbers.

Reconcile the claim before sending another reminder

  1. Check your side. Look at the bank or payment provider used on the invoice, recent incoming payments, pending items, fees, currency differences and any partial amount. Compare the invoice number with the payment references you have.
  2. Request traceable details. Ask for the actual send date, amount, method and reference or remittance advice. A payment scheduled for later is different from one already sent.
  3. Compare the records. Match the reference and amount against your account or ask your payment provider to help trace a payment you still cannot locate. Use the client's established contact route if any payment instructions need verification.
  4. Close the loop. Tell the client when the payment is matched. If it was not released, ask for the confirmed send date; if only part arrived, state the verified remaining balance.

FAQ

What do I say when a client says they already paid my invoice?

Thank them for the update and say you cannot match the payment in your records yet. Ask for the send date, amount and transaction reference or remittance advice. Compare those details with your bank or payment provider before sending another payment request.

Does a payment approval mean the money was sent?

Not necessarily. Ask whether the client means approved, scheduled or actually released, and request the expected send date if it has not left their system. Keep your invoice status based on what you can verify on your side.

What if the reference is real but I still cannot find the money?

Check the amount, currency, date and destination against your own records, including pending or unmatched payments. If it still does not match, ask your bank or payment provider how to trace it using the available reference. Tell the client what you are checking without asserting that their payment failed.
