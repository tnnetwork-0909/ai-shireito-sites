When the invoice did not reach the right person

Client Says They Never Received Your Invoice: Resend Email

If a client says they never received your invoice, confirm the correct recipient and resend the original invoice or its working link. Name the invoice number, amount and due date, then ask the client to confirm they can open it and tell you if their accounts team needs anything else. Do not assume they ignored the first message or issue a duplicate invoice.

Resend email you can use

Subject: Resending invoice [number] — please confirm receipt

Hi [client name],

Thanks for letting me know you could not find invoice [number]. I'm sending the original invoice again [attached / at the same secure link]. It shows [amount and currency] due on [date printed on the invoice].

Could you confirm that it arrived and that you can open it? If it needs to go to another address or your accounts team needs a purchase order number or other detail, please let me know so I can send it to the right place.

Once it is in the payment process, could you tell me the expected payment date?

Thanks,
[your name]

Keep only the attachment or link wording that is true. If the invoice is not yet due, the date is a useful reminder, not a reason to call the payment late. If a billing detail is wrong, correct it through your usual invoicing process before resending and make clear which document is current.

Resend it without creating confusion

  1. Check what was sent. Find the invoice number, original document, recipient address, due date and any delivery notice. A copy in your Sent folder does not show that the client could open it.
  2. Confirm the destination. Ask the client which address or payment portal their accounts team uses. Verify the recipient before attaching an invoice that contains client details.
  3. Send the same invoice again. Attach the original file or share its existing working link, unless you found an error that needs a corrected document. Use the email above and make the document easy to identify.
  4. Get a useful reply. Ask for confirmation that the invoice opens and whether an approval, purchase order number or other requirement is holding it up. Note the expected payment date once the client gives one.

FAQ

Should I make a new invoice number when I resend it?

Usually, resending means sending the same original invoice so the client can recognize it. If it contains an error, correct it using your normal invoicing process and explain which version is current. Avoid leaving two payable copies in circulation.

What should I say if my Sent folder shows the invoice email?

Say you are sending the invoice again, then ask the client to confirm that they can open it. A sent message alone does not settle whether the file reached the right person. Check the address and offer another agreed delivery route if needed.

Can I call the payment overdue after the client says the invoice was missing?

First check the agreed payment terms and whether the client received a usable invoice. You can state the date printed on the invoice as a fact, but avoid blaming the client for a delivery problem you have not resolved. Once receipt is clear, ask for the payment plan or use the overdue guide if it fits.
