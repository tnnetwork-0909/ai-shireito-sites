From invoice to draft

1. Add the details

Client name, your name, amount, currency, invoice number and due date. Client email and payment instructions are optional. Choose USD, GBP, EUR, CAD, AUD or NZD.

2. Choose timing and tone

Pick 3 days before, due today, 3 days late, 7 days late or 14 days late. Each has polite, standard and firm wording. At 10 days late, use the 7 days late stage.

3. Review and send yourself

Copy the draft and edit it in your email composer, or open a draft in your default email app and edit there. NudgePay does not send or schedule email.

Use Gmail in your browser? Copy the body into Gmail and use the subject shown in NudgePay. “Open in email app” uses your browser’s configured email handler; if nothing opens, use the copy buttons.

What a reminder can sound like

Example using the 7 days late, polite template with sample invoice details:

Subject: Checking in on overdue invoice INV-1042

Hi Alex,

I'm checking in about invoice INV-1042 for $1,800.00, which was due on Monday, October 12, 2026 and is still overdue.

Could you let me know when you expect to make the payment? If something is holding it up, I'm happy to talk it through.

Thanks so much,
Jordan

The draft uses your invoice details. Review and change the wording in your email composer before sending.

Your invoice details stay in your browser

The tool saves your entries in this browser so you can return to them. Use Clear in the tool to remove them. It has no account or AI service. Opening a draft hands its contents to your email app only when you choose that action.
