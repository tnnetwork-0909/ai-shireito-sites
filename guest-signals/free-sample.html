Guest Signals: Rings

Free napkin embroidery pattern · Connection · For someone you are glad is in your life.

1. Check the print

This square should measure 50 mm (about 2 in) on each side.

Wrong size? Reprint at 100% / Actual size, with scaling off.

2. Trace the sign

Align the solid top and left guides with the finished edges of one napkin corner. Trace only the two dark circles. The sign's center is 38 mm (about 1.5 in) from each edge.

The sign occupies a 30 × 30 mm (about 1.2 × 1.2 in) box. The pale lines are placement guides, not stitches.

3. Stitch

Use two strands of cotton embroidery floss and stem stitch both circles. Bring the needle up on the line. Take a 2–3 mm stitch and come up halfway back. Keep the working thread on the same side as you repeat.

Test a water-soluble fabric pen on a hidden spot first. Tape the sheet to a bright window, lay the napkin over it, trace, then stitch in a hoop. Remove marks as the pen maker directs.

4. Write the card

Cut along the dashed edge after printing.
